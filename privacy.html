Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 Nov 2026

This page explains what personal information Antarshanti Healing collects, why, and what you can do about it. We have tried to keep it plain and accurate.

Who we are

Antarshanti Healing is run by Srikanth from Hyderabad, India. The site is antarshanti.co.in. We offer online Jyotish (Vedic astrology) consultations, online Bach Flower therapy consultations, and a landing page for the Art of Living Happiness Program. There is also a free 5-minute wellbeing check-in on this site. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), Srikanth decides why and how your information is used.

What we collect and why

  • Wellbeing check-in. The check-in has 25 questions. It is a self-reflection tool, not a diagnosis. If you choose to save your result, we store your answers and scores along with the name and WhatsApp number you enter, and an email address if you choose to give one. We use this so that Srikanth can follow up if you ask him to, and so you can come back to your result.
  • How you found us. If a link you clicked carries campaign tags (utm_ values), we note them, along with the page you landed on and the website you came from (the domain only). We use this to understand which of our efforts reach people.
  • WhatsApp messages. If you message us, we see your number and what you write. For a Jyotish consultation, you share birth details with Srikanth on WhatsApp. They are not entered on this website.
  • Bach Flower payments. The Rs 1,000 consultation fee is paid online through Razorpay. Razorpay handles your card, UPI or bank details. This site never sees or stores them. We keep the order ID and payment ID that Razorpay gives us, so we can match a payment to a booking and process refunds.
  • Jyotish contributions. Jyotish is donation-based. If you choose to contribute by UPI after your session, you do it in your own UPI app. This site does not collect or process it.
  • Usage analytics. We may use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or Plausible to count page views and button clicks. These tools load only if we have switched them on. If they are on, they may set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser.

We do not store any health diagnosis about you. The check-in is not a medical tool.

Consent and how to withdraw it

We use your information because you gave it to us for a specific purpose, for example by pressing Save on the check-in, by messaging us, or by booking a consultation. You can withdraw your consent at any time by telling us on WhatsApp or by phone (see Contact). Withdrawing does not undo anything we did before you withdrew. We may still keep payment records if the law requires it.

Who sees your information

  • Srikanth, who runs the services.
  • Razorpay, which processes Bach Flower payments.
  • Supabase, the managed database service where we store saved check-in details and payment references.
  • Netlify, which hosts this website.
  • Analytics tools (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Plausible), only if we have switched them on.
  • WhatsApp, for messages you send us there, under its own terms.

We do not sell your information. We share it with others only as described above, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep saved check-in responses for up to 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Payment order and payment references are kept for as long as we need them for refunds, accounts and legal reasons. WhatsApp conversations stay in Srikanth's WhatsApp until you ask for them to be deleted or he clears them. After that, we delete or anonymise what we hold.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what personal information of yours we hold and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to erase your information;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • make a complaint to us and have it looked at. If you are not satisfied, you may take it to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these, contact us as described below. We may need to confirm it is really you before we act on a request.

Children

This website is not directed at anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please do not use the check-in or send us personal information yourself. Bach Flower consultations for a child are booked by a parent or guardian, who provides the information and gives consent on the child's behalf.

The check-in is not for emergencies

The wellbeing check-in is for self-reflection. It cannot respond to a crisis. If you are in distress or thinking of harming yourself, please contact someone right now:

  • Tele-MANAS: 14416
  • KIRAN helpline: 1800-599-0019
  • In an emergency, call 112.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will ask for fresh consent where the law requires it.
