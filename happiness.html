Common questions

Before you join

Do I need prior experience in yoga or meditation?

None at all. The program is designed for complete beginners as much as seasoned practitioners. Just show up with an open mind for all three days.

Is it religious?

No. The Happiness Program draws from Vedic wisdom but is completely secular. People of all faiths — and none — participate and benefit equally.

How long is each session?

Each of the three days runs approximately 3–4 hours. Online sessions are in morning or evening slots based on the batch schedule.

What happens after the program?

You receive the SKY practice to do daily at home. Follow-up long-SKY sessions are available through Art of Living centres. I remain available on WhatsApp for support.
