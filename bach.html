Common questions

Before your first session

Are Bach Flower Remedies safe?

Yes — completely. Diluted flower essences preserved in brandy. No side effects, no drug interactions, safe for children, pregnant women and the elderly.

How long before I notice a difference?

Some people feel a shift within days. For deeper emotional patterns, 4–6 weeks of taking the remedy is typical. A follow-up consultation helps refine the blend.

How is the remedy chosen?

Through a detailed conversation about your current emotional state — what you feel, when, and how it affects daily life. The remedy is always personalised.

Can I take them alongside medication?

Yes. Bach Flower Remedies are complementary — they do not interfere with any medical treatment.
