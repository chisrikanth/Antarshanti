What is Jyotish?

The oldest living system of self-knowledge

Planetary positions reveal personality, relationships, career, health, and timing of major events through planetary periods called dashas.

Common questions

Before you book

Do I need to know my exact birth time?

The more precise the birth time, the more accurate the chart. Share what you have — I apply rectification techniques to narrow it down if needed.

Is Jyotish about predicting the future?

Not quite. Jyotish reveals tendencies, timing and karma — free will always plays a role. The reading helps you understand your current chapter and navigate it wisely.

How is this different from Sun-sign horoscopes?

Completely different. Vedic astrology uses your exact birth chart across 16 divisional charts, planetary periods, and classical techniques. A Sun-sign column uses one of dozens of factors.

What do I share after booking?

Date of birth, exact time of birth, and place of birth — sent over WhatsApp after payment. You can also share the main area of life you want to focus on.
